Assemblies & Collective Orders

Assemblies & Collective Orders

An assembly is represented as a collective order whose component parts arrive as individual order lines sharing the same grouping value.

  • Multiple order lines can belong to one collective order when they are connected — an assembly is one collective order, and its parts are the component orders. The grouping value is carried in the Assembly / CollectiveOrder fields and is used as the job number.

  • When importing an assembly through spreadsheet import, the assembly (and its collective-order representation) is created without spawning a spurious empty job tile — importing must not create a data-less job.

Warning

Known pitfall (resolved). Previously, importing an assembly via spreadsheet import while unmapping the Part Order field and enabling “import unmapped PPS field as external field” could create an empty job tile, and opening that job’s details raised a database error. The correct behavior is: assembly import creates the assembly only; no empty job is created and job details load cleanly.
